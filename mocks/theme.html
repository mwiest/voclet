Voclet — theme reference

A mirror of the app's live theme, not a proposal. Every hex comes from ui/theme/Color.kt and every mock paints the roles the real screens paint, so a screen that looks wrong here looks wrong on the tablet. Keep this file in sync when colours or role usage change — the role map at the bottom lists what to touch.

One rule runs through the practice screens: word1, the prompt in the language you know, is tertiaryContainer; word2, the answer you are practising, is primaryContainer. Connect, Flashcards and Fill Blanks all follow it, so a learner reads the same two tints as "asked" and "answered" everywhere. Green and red stay reserved for right and wrong.
