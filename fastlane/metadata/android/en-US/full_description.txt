Voclet helps you learn vocabulary with short, playful practice. Make word lists by typing, by importing a file, or by photographing a page of your workbook, then practise them in four ways.

<b>Four ways to practise</b>
• <b>Connect:</b> draw lines between words and their translations
• <b>Flashcards:</b> flip a card, then say whether you knew it
• <b>Fill the blank:</b> drag the missing letters into the word
• <b>Spell it:</b> type the word; a letter-by-letter comparison shows what went wrong

Words are read aloud with your device's text-to-speech voices.

<b>Focus on what is hard</b>
• Practise one list, several at once, or only the pairs you starred
• "Hard words" picks the pairs you got wrong recently

<b>Build lists quickly</b>
• Photograph a vocabulary page, mark the area, and the word pairs are added to your list for review
• Get translation suggestions while you type
• Import pairs from CSV files; share lists and back them up as files

<b>AI on your terms</b>
Camera import and translation suggestions are optional. They can run entirely on your device with small models you download in Settings, or use a cloud AI provider with your own API key: Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral, or any compatible server. Voclet has no server of its own.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your lists stay on your device
• Free and open-source software (Apache 2.0)

<b>Languages</b>
Word lists in English, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish and Hungarian, in any combination. The app itself is available in the same thirteen languages.

Made for tablets, works on phones. Light and dark themes.
